Intended use: This tool is for research and educational demonstration only and does not constitute medical advice; final clinical decisions must be made by clinicians using the full clinical picture.
Model: ExtraTrees (ranger, extratrees split; 500 trees; node-level export; in-browser inference matches the R implementation).
Benchmark performance: Temporal validation cohort AUROC 0.758; eICU-CRD external model-selection cohort AUROC 0.765. Scorecard AUROC: internal 0.733, temporal 0.718, eICU-CRD 0.742.
Runtime: The hosted version loads forest.bin.gz and meta.json from the same directory. All calculations run locally in the browser; no patient data are uploaded or stored.
